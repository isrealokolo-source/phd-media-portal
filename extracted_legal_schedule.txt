Cost Approval and Recoupment
1. Only costs permitted by the Agreement and approved in writing where required shall qualify as Recoupable Costs.
2. Each claimed cost must be supported by reasonable documentary evidence, including an invoice, receipt, payment record or other appropriate record.
3. Expenditure exceeding an approved budget or introducing a new material expense category requires prior written approval in accordance with the Agreement.
4. Approved Recoupable Costs and Advances shall be recouped through the revenue waterfall provided in the Agreement before the 70% Artist / 30% PHD allocation.
5. PHD shall not deduct the same expense twice. Ordinary management and distribution services included in PHD’s 30% share shall not be charged separately unless expressly agreed in writing.
6. An unrecouped balance shall be treated in accordance with the Agreement and shall not automatically become the Artist’s personal debt.

GENERAL SCHEDULE TERMS
1. Incorporation: Schedules A and B form part of the Agreement once duly approved and incorporated by the Parties.
2. Ownership: Nothing in either Schedule transfers copyright ownership to PHD. The Artist or other lawful owner retains ownership as identified in the applicable register.
3. Scope of control: PHD’s distribution, management and exploitation authority is limited to the Masters accepted under the Agreement and remains subject to its duration, approval requirements and other terms.
4. Changes: Additions or material changes to a Master’s ownership, rights status or scope must be documented in writing and approved in accordance with the Agreement.
5. Conflicts: If a Schedule conflicts with the main Agreement, the main Agreement prevails unless the Parties expressly agree in writing to vary the relevant provision.
6. Records: Each Party shall retain relevant approvals, licences, clearances and expenditure records in accordance with the Agreement and applicable law
